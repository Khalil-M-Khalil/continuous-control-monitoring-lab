# Continuous Control Monitoring & Board Risk Lab

## Chosen direction: Board Signal Room

A practical executive GRC simulation where the user converts control telemetry into a board-ready risk decision. The interface uses an editorial risk cockpit: calm paper background, graphite text, cobalt evidence, oxide action, and clay escalation.

The user reviews six KPIs across Access, Logging, Vulnerability, Vendor, Backup, and Policy domains. They inspect trend, target, owner, overdue actions, and evidence confidence; select a response (accept, remediate, escalate), then produce an executive brief. The lab distinguishes a metric from a decision: an amber KPI only becomes meaningful when its business consequence and accountable action are explicit.

The model is informed by NIST SP 800-137 continuous monitoring, NIST CSF 2.0 Govern/Identify/Protect/Detect/Respond/Recover concepts, and COSO ERM governance and reporting context. All data is fictional and the app makes no external connections.

References: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/137/final ; https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework ; https://www.coso.org/guidance-erm
